> In the code collection, the forward files calculate and output the forward reconstruction errors and PDE residual losses for 1W and 2W solitons under different network architectures, sampling strategies, and training configurations.
> Under the inverse directory, the noise files include the cubic-coefficient identification for 1W and 2W solitons under different coefficient initializations and observation-noise levels. The derivative-dependent operator, the saturable operator, and the quintic operator correspond to Case A, B, and C, respectively. The three files respectively present the recovered operator-conditioned coefficients under cubic-coefficient misspecification.
